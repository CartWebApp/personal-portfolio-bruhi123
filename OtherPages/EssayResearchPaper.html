Research Paper: “The Importance of Animal Well-Being”

Concepts Learned

  • Research: We asked AI for sources of information for our essay. Then we analyzed them and took notes from it
  • Increase of vocabulary: I search up synonyms of words that sounded weak to improve my vocabulary
  • Structure of an essay: It all starts with an introduction, then a piece of evidence, another piece of evidence, then a counter argument, finally a conclusion

Habits of Mind in Action

  • Persistence: After I realized that I had a lot of grammar spelling mistakes, I decided to have some hours to correct my mistakes to have the best results
  • Thinking and Communicating with Clarity and Precision: I tried to avoid vague language to have the best results in my essay

Challenges & Solutions

  • The Problem: A major problem was time management. This impacted a lot because sometimes I procrastinate and finish my stuff on the last day
  • The Solution: I hopefully finished on time since I decided to organize my time schedule to not fall behind
  • The Problem: I did not know the difference between paraphrasing and summarizing
  • The Solution: My classmates and I did assignments that helped us understand what is the difference and how to build them.
  • The Problem: Since English is my second language it was difficult to write fast enough
  • The Solution: I worked on it outside of class to finish the writing I was missing to catch up.
