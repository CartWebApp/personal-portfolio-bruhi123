Budget Calculator

This is a budget calculator that allows users to input their income and expenses, and then calculates the total budget and displays it on the screen.

Concepts Learned

  • Overlays: I learned this by asking Mateo on how to do them, thanks to him I used them on the goal management Figma wireframes
  • Prototyping Dropdowns: I learned at the end of this project that there was an easier way to build drop downs; you just had to make a variant to make them connect

Challenges & Solutions

  • The Problem: I struggled doing dropdowns and I did a longer way to do them
  • The Solution: After my teacher taught me how to build it in an easier way I deleted the long way and simplified it
  • The Problem: All my teammates dropped out of CART
  • The Solution: I joined another team and I adapted quickly which helped me on my final presentation
  • The Problem:Time management was a big issue for me since I got activities through my day that occupies a lot of my time
  • The Solution: I tried to organize myself to finish everything before the deadline

Habits of Mind

  • Persistence: There was an error in the prototyping flow, I tried fixing it for a while until I asked my teacher about it. He helped me realize why the error was caused which helped me learn how prototyping works.
  • Communication: I sometimes lack communication with my partner and it was me ending up doing all the Figma.
